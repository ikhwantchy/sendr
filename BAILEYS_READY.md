## 📱 **CONNECT KE WHATSAPP!**

### **STEP 1: Refresh Browser**
- Tekan **F5**

### **STEP 2: Generate QR**
1. Go to **Bots** page
2. Click **"Manage"** on your bot
3. Click **"Connect to WhatsApp"**
4. **QR Code akan muncul CEPAT!** ⚡

### **STEP 3: Scan QR**
1. **Buka WhatsApp di HP**
2. **Menu → Linked Devices → Link a Device**
3. **SCAN QR CODE!** 📱
4. **Status berubah "Connected"!** ✅
